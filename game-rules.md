# Official Game Rules — Ludo Arena

## 1. Board Anatomy & Token Deployment

1. **Board Grid**: The Ludo Arena board is a symmetrical 15×15 grid containing:
   - 4 Corner Yards (Red, Green, Yellow, Blue).
   - 52 Outer Track squares forming a continuous circular loop.
   - 4 Colored Home Columns (5 squares each) leading into the center triumph.
   - 1 Central Triumph Zone with four colored finish triangles.
   - 8 Safe Star Squares that provide immunity from captures.

2. **Pawn Deployment (Exiting the Yard)**:
   - Each player begins with 4 pawns inside their respective colored yard.
   - By default, a player must roll a **6** to deploy a pawn from the yard onto their starting track square.
   - In customizable room rules, this can be configured to allow either a **1 or 6**.
   - If a player rolls a value that does not allow deployment and has no active pawns on the track, their turn ends immediately.

---

## 2. Movement & Path Traversal

1. **Clockwise Traversal**: Once deployed onto the starting track square, pawns advance clockwise along the 52 perimeter squares according to the rolled dice value.
2. **Turn Sequence**: Turn order proceeds clockwise in the sequence: **Red ➔ Green ➔ Yellow ➔ Blue**.
3. **Pawn Stacking**: Multiple pawns (of the same color or different colors) may occupy the same track square. When multiple pawns occupy a square, they are visually offset so all tokens remain selectable.

---

## 3. Captures, Strikes & Safe Zones

1. **Capturing Opponents**:
   - If a pawn ends its move on a normal track square already occupied by an opponent's pawn, the opponent's pawn is **captured** (struck).
   - The captured pawn is immediately returned to its owner's corner yard and must wait for another 6 to re-enter.
   - The capturing player is immediately awarded a **Bonus Turn**.

2. **Safe Star Squares (Capture Immunity)**:
   - There are **8 Safe Star Squares** on the track:
     - 4 Start Squares: Red (Cell 1), Green (Cell 14), Yellow (Cell 27), Blue (Cell 40).
     - 4 Mid-Arm Star Squares: Cells 9, 22, 35, and 48.
   - Pawns resting on any of these 8 star squares **cannot be captured**. Opponent pawns can co-exist on a star square in complete safety.

---

## 4. Home Stretch & Center Triumph

1. **Home Path Entry**:
   - After traversing all 52 squares of the main track, a pawn turns into its color-matched 5-square Home Column.
   - Opponents can never enter another player's Home Column; pawns in the Home Column are 100% immune from capture.

2. **Center Triumph (Exact Roll Requirement)**:
   - To finish, a pawn must reach the central triumph square (Step 56) with an **exact dice roll**.
   - For example, if a pawn is on the 4th square of the Home Column (Step 54), it requires an exact roll of **2** to enter the center. A roll of 3 or higher is illegal and cannot be applied to that pawn.

---

## 5. Bonus Turns & Penalties

1. **Rolling a 6**:
   - Rolling a 6 earns the player an immediate extra roll.
2. **Striking an Opponent**:
   - Successfully capturing an opponent's pawn grants an immediate extra roll.
3. **Finishing a Pawn**:
   - Bringing a pawn into the central triumph grants an immediate extra roll.
4. **Three Consecutive Sixes Penalty**:
   - If a player rolls three consecutive 6s in a single turn, the 3rd roll is forfeited and the turn passes to the next player immediately.

---

## 6. Two-versus-Two (2v2) Team Ludo Rules

1. **Team Pairings**:
   - Default **Team Alpha**: Red (Player 1) + Yellow (Player 3).
   - Default **Team Omega**: Green (Player 2) + Blue (Player 4).
   - Before the match starts, players may request a team swap with a connected player on the opposing team in the room lobby. The invited player must accept; seat colors and turn order remain unchanged.
2. **Partner Immunity**:
   - Teammates **cannot capture each other**. Landing on a teammate's square simply creates a shared cooperative stack.
3. **Team Victory Condition**:
   - A team wins when both partners successfully bring all **8 pawns** into the center triumph.
